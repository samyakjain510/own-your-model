# Writing rules (for humans and agents)

Applies to everything public: README, docs, dataset and model cards, the report, the deck.

- First person, plain language a teenager could follow.
- Never use "this isn't X, it's Y", "X instead of Y", or paired-sentence versions of those contrasts.
- No forced groups of three. No stock AI words (delve, leverage, robust, seamless, crucial, landscape, unlock, game-changer, etc.).
- No invented numbers or stories. Every number points to a saved run in `runs/` or a cited source with a link and date.
- Say who did what: label Claude-made labels and agent work as such. Never imply a human checked something they didn't.
- No mention of any prior school project or its sponsor. No retail or travel examples.
